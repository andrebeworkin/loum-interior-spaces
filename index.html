THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIECES

Hello,
I'm Louisa.

For me, a love of interiors began at home.

Growing up, my father invited us to help decorate every house we lived in. Our home was warm, beautiful and intentional. It was where I felt safest, and where my creativity was encouraged.

That feeling is at the heart of Loum Interior Spaces. I want to help you find the same warmth, joy and sense of belonging in your own home—through spaces that reflect your culture, heritage and personality.

Louisa Mallam
